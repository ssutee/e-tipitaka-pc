# E-Tipitaka — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 June 2026_

E-Tipitaka is a desktop application for reading the Thai Tipitaka. This policy
explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your computer only: your bookmarks,
notes, reading history, and app settings, plus copies of the scripture
databases. This data stays on your device unless you choose to use the optional
cloud backup feature.

## Optional account

The app works fully without an account. If you choose to create one, we collect
the **email address, username, and password** you provide, sent to our server
at `data.etipitaka.com` to register and sign you in. Your login token is stored
on your device and removed when you log out.

## Optional cloud backup

If you are signed in and choose to back up, the app uploads your personal data
file (bookmarks, notes, settings) to `data.etipitaka.com` so you can restore it
on another device. You can list, download, and delete these backups from within
the app at any time.

## Database updates

The app downloads scripture text and database updates over HTTPS from
watnapahpong.org. These are **downloads only** — no personal data is sent.

## What we do NOT do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no third-party data sharing, and no
selling of your data.

## Data retention and deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app or delete it in-app. Cloud
backups are deleted when you delete them in-app. To delete your account, contact
us at the address below.

## Children

The app is a religious-text reader and is not directed at children. It does not
knowingly collect data from children.

## Contact

Questions or data requests: **etipitaka@gmail.com**
